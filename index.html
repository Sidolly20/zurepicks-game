What are 2 odds daily tips?

2 odds daily is a premium slip of two selections that together pay roughly double your stake. It is a popular way to bet because it keeps the slip short: two matches to follow, not twelve. Members see the two selections as soon as they are published, along with the odds for each.

How a 2 odds slip works

You pick two matches and combine them into one bet (an accumulator, also called a double). The odds multiply, so two selections at 1.40 and 1.45 give combined odds of about 2.03. The bet only wins if both selections win, which is why choosing safer markets matters more than chasing a big price.

Why we publish a 7-day record

Plenty of tipster sites show only their wins. We show the last seven days of 2 Odds results on the home page, losses included. A short, public, day-by-day record is the simplest honest way to see whether a tipster is worth following.

Popular betting markets explained

Both teams to score (BTTS)

BTTS, also written GG, pays when each side scores at least once. It suits open matches between attacking teams or sides with shaky defences. No Goal (NG) is the opposite: at least one team fails to score.

Over 2.5 goals and under 2.5 goals

Over 2.5 wins when the match has three or more goals; under 2.5 wins with two or fewer. Lines like over 1.5 and over 3.5 work the same way and move the risk up or down. Over 1.5 goals is one of the most common choices for safer slips.

Double chance: 1X, X2 and 12

Double chance covers two of the three outcomes. 1X is home win or draw, X2 is away win or draw, and 12 is either team to win. The odds are lower, but so is the risk.

Draw no bet

Back one team and get your stake back if the match is drawn. It sits between a straight win bet and a double chance bet.
