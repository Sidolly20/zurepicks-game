Responsible gambling

ZurePicks shares football information and opinions. We do not take bets and we are not a bookmaker. Betting is for people aged 18 and over, and it is only legal in some places, so check your local law first.

Keep it in control

  • Decide your budget before you bet and stick to it.
  • Never bet money you need for food, rent, school fees or bills.
  • Do not chase losses. A bad day is not fixed by a bigger bet.
  • No tip is guaranteed. Treat every pick as one opinion.

Warning signs

Betting more than you planned, lying about it, borrowing to bet, or feeling anxious when you are not betting are all signs to stop and talk to someone. Most bookmakers offer deposit limits and self-exclusion, and many countries have free gambling support lines.
