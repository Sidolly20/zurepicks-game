About the 2 odds daily slip

What you get

Each day we publish two selections chosen for lower risk, such as double chance, over 1.5 goals or a strong favourite to win. Combined, the odds land around 2.00.

Why 2 odds is a popular target

A short slip needs fewer things to go right than a long accumulator. That does not make it safe, but it makes the maths easier to understand and the record easier to check.

Are the football betting tips on ZurePicks free?

Yes. Every free tip for today, tomorrow and the days ahead is on the home page with no sign-up. Only the daily 2 Odds page is premium.

What are 2 odds daily tips?

2 Odds is a short daily slip of two selections, published for premium members. Each selection is a single match with one tip, and the slip is settled as a win only when both selections win.

How much does premium cost?

Premium is 5 USD per 7 days. You register, pay, and the admin activates your account. Access ends automatically when the 7 days are over.

Can you guarantee a win?

No. Nobody can. Football is unpredictable, so every tip carries risk. That is why we publish the last 7 days of 2 Odds results, wins and losses alike.
